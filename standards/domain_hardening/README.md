# Domain Hardening Program

This directory tracks the promotion of the remaining M1 domains into independently checked decision models.

The program does not promote a workbook because it has more formulas, tabs, or visual polish. A domain advances only when its financial identities, boundary conditions, stress behavior, and decision outputs are independently testable.

## M1 cohort

- 08 Asset Management
- 10 Trade Finance
- 11 Microfinance
- 12 Equity Finance
- 15 Commodities
- 16 Crypto & Digital Assets
- 17 Real Estate & REIT
- 23 Fintech & Payments
- 24 Distressed & Restructuring

## Required hardening sequence

1. Bind each domain to primary standards, regulator guidance, or authoritative market conventions.
2. Define independent reference identities that do not reuse workbook formulas.
3. Add adversarial boundary tests and monotonicity tests.
4. Add workbook contracts for required sheets, outputs, checks, and failure states.
5. Add one conventional and one adversarial benchmark instance.
6. Promote to M2 only after all gates pass.
