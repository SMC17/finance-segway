## Rejected during reconciliation

- Wholesale merge of the prototype branch
- Prototype binary workbook objects that did not match the canonical synthesis history
- A shared Private Credit / Debt Finance workbook after the domains developed different decisions and mechanics
- Connector-side binary transport
- Automated workflows that commit routine refresh reports
- Promotion to M3 without model cards, independent validation records, public instances, and outcome monitoring

## Merge blockers

- Synthesis CI and both independent-oracle suites remain green on the human-authored head.
- No workbook contains external links or literal Excel errors.
- The inventory remains complete and internally consistent.
- No model is promoted above its evidence.
- At least one flagship receives a complete model card, source register, independent validation record, reviewed release artifact, and public reference/adversarial instance pair.
- The owner explicitly approves promotion from draft to merge-ready.
