# Domain Max Policy — Service Layer Ambition

**Status**: Binding  
**Related**: `docs/AI_NATIVE_GOLDMAN_ROADMAP.md`, `standards/model_inventory.json`

## End state

Every core domain (all 24) is taken to the maximum maturity the evidence supports — ultimately M4 where public instances and outcome monitoring are feasible. That is the service layer: any governed product we sell (private credit underwriting, LBO advisory, restructuring screens, risk packs, etc.) is backed by a maintained, auditable archetype.

## Sequencing (how we get there without diluting trust)

Breadth at M2 is already done. Depth is expensive (real instances, source registers, RefreshLog discipline, outcome comparisons, agent tools).

Therefore:

1. **Flagships first** — the six domains in the AI-native Goldman roadmap receive M3/M4 evidence, real instances, and L3 tool contracts first. These are the ones that generate near-term service revenue and the end-to-end demo.
2. **Remaining domains** stay solid M2 (correct skeleton, reference checks, institutional surface). They are upgraded to M3/M4 when:
   - a client engagement requires them, or
   - a flagship depends on them, or
   - capacity exists after flagship evidence packs are green.
3. **No fake maturity** — inventory claims stay honest. Synthetic-only instances do not count toward M3/M4.

## Service-layer implication

When we say we provide a service layer “of everything we stated,” we mean:

- The library is complete enough that an agent or analyst can pick the right archetype.
- Flagship archetypes are deep enough to produce a client-grade Excel receipt with Sources, Checks, and RefreshLog.
- Non-flagship archetypes are not abandoned; they are queued for the same depth under the same standards.

Spreading M3 evidence thinly across all 24 at once produces 24 weak packs and zero reliable agents. Concentrating on six produces a credible service surface and a template the other eighteen can copy.

## Research rails

RAM, kdb, and dbt remain sidecars. They feed dated inputs into governed models via source registers. They do not receive M-maturity ratings and do not replace domain engines.
