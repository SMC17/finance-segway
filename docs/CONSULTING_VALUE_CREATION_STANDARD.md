# Consulting Value-Creation Standard

## Purpose

The value-creation layer connects operational redesign to financial outcomes
without turning a point estimate into a promise. Every bridge is assumption-led,
dependency-aware, evidence-linked, and challengeable from activity economics
through exit equity value.

## Underwriting stack

1. **Observed baseline** — establish volume, cycle time, labor, capacity,
   quality loss, working capital, and authoritative evidence before redesign.
2. **Deterministic case** — calculate labor, revenue, loss avoidance, recurring
   cost, implementation cost, NPV, payback, risk, and dependencies.
3. **Uncertainty case** — declare low/mode/high distributions for cost, value,
   adoption, delivery, and working-capital release plus a feasibility
   probability. Preserve the seed and sample checksum.
4. **Capacity case** — test arrivals, stage servers, service-time distributions,
   queueing, utilization, and rework so a proposed gain does not move the
   bottleneck.
5. **Portfolio bridge** — translate realized revenue through incremental gross
   margin, add labor and other savings, subtract recurring cost, and separately
   track one-time cost, capex, and working-capital release.
6. **Investment bridge** — apply the declared exit multiple to EBITDA, update
   net debt for cash effects, and calculate enterprise value, equity value,
   MOIC, and IRR against the same entry equity and horizon.
7. **100-day sequence** — validate dependencies, compute earliest start and
   finish, expose the critical path, and block unresolved decision gates whose
   evidence is absent.
8. **Outcome measurement** — freeze the baseline and target, name the owner,
   record guardrails, bridge forecast to actual, and label attribution limits.

## Double-counting controls

- Revenue uplift contributes to EBITDA only through incremental gross margin.
- Labor and other savings are separate named drivers.
- Recurring cost reduces EBITDA even when benefit realization is partial.
- One-time cost and capex consume cash and increase net debt relative to the
  otherwise identical exit case; they do not also reduce annual EBITDA.
- Working-capital release reduces net debt and is not counted as EBITDA.
- Initiative identifiers are unique and dependencies must exist and be acyclic.
- Every initiative carries evidence identifiers; a result without source
  identity is not a governed value bridge.

## Attribution boundary

Pre/post movement is not automatically caused by an intervention.
Difference-in-differences removes the observed change in a comparison group but
still depends on design assumptions. The engine therefore reports a descriptive
comparison by default. A controlled estimate requires documented assumptions
such as reviewed parallel trends and absence of differential concurrent
interventions. Accountable review remains outside the arithmetic.

## Maturity

The deterministic uncertainty and portfolio-company engines are A1. A2 requires
a frozen, source-addressed real operating case, reproducible workflow receipts,
and independent review. Realization and attribution remain A1 until governed
real outcomes exist. No test vector constitutes client evidence, an investment
approval, or a maintained production outcome system.
